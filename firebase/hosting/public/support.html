Support

SMMP (Social Media Mini-Platform) · iOS app

Need help with SMMP? This page covers how to reach us and answers to common questions.

Email support
vlad.arsenyuk@gmail.com

In the app: open Profile → menu (⋯) → Contact.

Getting started

  • Create an account: Register with email and password on the login screen.
  • Find people: Use the Search tab to find users and tap Follow.
  • See posts: Your Feed tab shows posts from people you follow.
  • Post: Tap the create tab to publish text and an optional photo.

Account & profile

  • Edit profile: Profile → Edit — change display name, bio, or profile photo.
  • Password reset: On the login screen, tap Forgot Password and enter your email.
  • Delete account: Profile → menu (⋯) → Delete Account. Enter your password to confirm. This permanently removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, follows, and images.

Safety & privacy

  • Report content: Use Report on another user’s post, comment, or profile.
  • Block a user: Open their profile → Block. Unblock from the same screen.
  • Privacy Policy: Read our Privacy Policy
  • Terms of Use: Read our Terms of Use

Offline mode

SMMP caches your feed and profiles locally. Without internet, you can still browse cached content. Creating posts, reporting, blocking, and account changes require a connection.

Response time

We aim to reply to support emails within a few business days. Reports of objectionable content are reviewed as soon as possible, typically within 24 hours.
