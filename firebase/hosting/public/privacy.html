Privacy Policy

SMMP (Social Media Mini-Platform) · iOS app
Operator: Vladyslav Arseniuk · Bundle ID: com.arseniuk.smmp
Last updated: September 19, 2026

Summary: SMMP is a social networking app. We collect account and content data needed to run the app. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites. You can delete your account inside the app at any time.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SMMP collects, how we use it, who can see it, and what choices you have. By creating an account, you agree to this policy and our Terms of Use.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following data when you use SMMP:

Data type Examples Purpose
Email address Used to sign in and reset your password Account creation and authentication
Name Display name on your profile Profile and social features
User ID Firebase Authentication UID Identify your account and link your content
Photos Profile photo and images attached to posts Profile and posting features (only when you choose a photo)
Other user content Posts, comments, likes, bio, follow list, blocks, reports Core social networking functionality and safety

All data listed above is linked to your identity and used for app functionality only. We do not use it for tracking or advertising.

2. What we do not collect

  • Precise location
  • Contacts from your address book
  • Health, financial, or payment information
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data
  • Analytics or crash-reporting SDK data (not used in this app)

3. Device permissions

Photo Library: SMMP asks for access only when you choose to attach a photo to a post or set a profile picture. We do not access your photo library in the background.

4. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account (sign up, sign in, password reset)
  • Show your profile, feed, posts, comments, and search results
  • Let you follow users, like posts, and publish content
  • Filter objectionable text, process reports, and enforce blocks
  • Cache feed and profile data on your device for offline browsing
  • Delete your data when you use in-app account deletion

5. What other users can see

SMMP is a social app. Other signed-in users can see:

  • Your display name, bio, profile photo, follower/following counts
  • Posts and comments you publish
  • Your likes (as part of post interaction counts)

Your email address is not shown on your public profile document. It is used only for authentication.

6. Third-party services

SMMP uses Google Firebase to provide:

  • Firebase Authentication — account sign-up and sign-in
  • Cloud Firestore — profiles, posts, comments, follows, reports, blocks
  • Cloud Storage — profile and post images

Data is stored on Google’s servers. Google’s privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We do not share your data with any other third parties for marketing.

7. User-generated content and safety

You may publish text and photos. SMMP includes a text filter, reporting, and blocking tools. If you report content, we store the report (reason, target, timestamp) to review it. We aim to review reports and remove violating content within 24 hours.

8. Data stored on your device

SMMP caches feed and profile data locally (Core Data) so you can browse when offline. This cache is removed when you delete the app.

9. How to delete your account and data

You can delete your account entirely inside the app:

  1. Open Profile
  2. Tap the menu (⋯) → Delete Account
  3. Confirm and enter your password

This permanently deletes:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your profile document in Firestore
  • Your posts, comments, likes, follows, and blocks
  • Your profile photo and post images in Storage

Reports you submitted may be kept for moderation records. You can also email vlad.arsenyuk@gmail.com with deletion requests.

10. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. After account deletion, associated personal data is removed from Firebase as described above.

11. Tracking and advertising

SMMP does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. SMMP does not display ads. Our App Store privacy label and in-app privacy manifest declare no tracking.

12. Children’s privacy

SMMP is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

We use Firebase security rules so users can only write their own profile, posts, and blocks. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored in plain text by SMMP. No online service is 100% secure.

14. International users

Your data may be processed on servers operated by Google in countries where Firebase operates. By using SMMP, you consent to this processing. If you are in the EEA or UK, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion). Contact us to exercise those rights.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of SMMP after an update means you accept the revised policy. The current version is always available in the app (Login and Profile screens) and at this URL.
